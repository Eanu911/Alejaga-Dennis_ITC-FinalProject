🥇 The World's Greatest Estate Developer

Tags: Comedy, Reincarnation, Transmigration, System
Chapter Count: 223 (Completed)
Author(s): BK_Moon
Artist(s): Kim Hyunsoo

When civil engineering student Suho Kim falls asleep reading a fantasy novel, he wakes up as a character in the book! Suho is now in the body of Lloyd Frontera, a lazy noble who loves to drink, and whose family is in a mountain of debt. Using his engineering knowledge, Suho designs inventions to avert the terrible future that lies in wait for him. With the help of a giant hamster, a knight, and the world’s magic, can Suho dig his new family out of debt and build a better future?

🥈 Her Summon

Tags: Fantasy, Transmigration, Superpowers
Chapter Count: 117 (Completed)
Author(s): Park Jinjoon
Artist(s): Park Jinjoon

Jin-Kyung is a reclusive man who spends most of his time playing games and avoiding the outside world. One day, he is unexpectedly summoned to another world by a powerful sorceress. However, instead of being a typical hero, Jin-Kyung possesses extraordinary abilities and an unusual appearance in this fantasy world. As he encounters monsters, powerful enemies, and unfamiliar challenges, he begins a journey that pushes him beyond his comfort zone and changes the way he sees himself and the world around him.
