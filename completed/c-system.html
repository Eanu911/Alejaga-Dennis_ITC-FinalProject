🥇 Solo Leveling

Tags: Fantasy, System, Gate, Dungeon
Chapter Count: 200 (Completed)
Author(s): Chu-Gong
Artist(s): Gi So-Ryeong, Jang Sung-Rak

In a world where hunters, human warriors who possess magical abilities, must battle deadly monsters to protect mankind from certain annihilation, a notoriously weak hunter named Sung Jinwoo finds himself in a seemingly endless struggle for survival. One day, after narrowly surviving an overwhelmingly powerful dungeon that nearly wipes out his entire party, a mysterious program called the System chooses him as its sole player and in turn, gives him the extremely rare ability to level up in strength, possibly beyond any known limits. Follow Jinwoo's journey as he fights against all kinds of enemies, both man and monster, to discover the secrets of the dungeons and the true source of his powers.

🥈 Questism

Tags: Action, System, Martial Arts
Chapter Count: 173 (Completed)
Author(s): Park Taejun, YuNuni
Artist(s): Park Taejun, TAEWAN

Gaming geek Suhyeon Kim hates school, and the dislike is mutual. Every day he faces relentless bullying from his classmates, leading him to wish his life was more like the RPG quest games he plays. Suhyeon’s wish is granted when a quest prompt pops up in real life with an easy quest and an enticing reward. With nothing to lose, he takes the chance, and one quest soon leads to another. As the stakes get higher and the rewards get bigger, Suhyeon soon finds he’s gone from being an ostracized nobody to the top of the school. Just how far will Suhyeon take his quests, and how far will he fall if he refuses to participate?

🥉 Player

Tags: Fantasy, System, Tower, Transmigration, Swordsmanship
Chapter Count: 264 (Completed)
Author(s): Jong-Seok Park
Artist(s): Hyeon-Jun Oh

Seoljin is a total loser at school: he's despised by everyone, even those he thought were his friends. The only thing he can do to escape this harsh reality is read action webtoons, imagining himself in the place of the heroes who bravely fight their opponents without the slightest hesitation. But Seoljin's life is about to take a completely different turn when he leaves a small comment on the webtoon "Player": a comment that will soon lead him into the very world of that webtoon!
