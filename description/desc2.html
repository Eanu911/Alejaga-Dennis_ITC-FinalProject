REGRESSION

Regression manhwa are Korean webtoons where the main character travels back in time to a past version of themselves after dying, failing, or suffering a devastating loss. Armed with future knowledge, they use past events, secret treasures, and foreknowledge to rewrite destiny.

TRANSMIGRATION

Transmigration manhwa (Korean comics) is a story where a character is transported into another body or world, often becoming a character from a novel, game, or fictional setting. They usually retain their memories and knowledge from their previous life.

REINCARNATION

Reincarnation manhwa is a story where a character is born again into a new life, either in the same world or another world. They often retain memories of their past life, which influence their decisions and actions in their new life.

SYSTEM

System manhwa features a game-like system that gives characters quests, stats, skills, and rewards, allowing them to become stronger by completing missions and leveling up.

MAGIC ACADEMY

Magic academy manhwa focuses on characters studying magic in specialized schools or academies, where they undergo magical training, participate in competitions, and discover new abilities.

SWORDSMANSHIP

Swordsmanship manhwa centers on characters who practice sword fighting and develop their skills through training and battles, often featuring sword masters, powerful techniques, and rivalries.

APOCALYPTIC

Apocalyptic manhwa takes place in a world devastated by disasters, wars, monsters, or other catastrophic events, where characters struggle to survive and rebuild society.

SLICE OF LIFE

Slice of life manhwa focuses on the everyday experiences, relationships, and personal growth of characters, often portraying realistic and relatable situations.

SUPERPOWERS

Superpowers manhwa features characters with extraordinary abilities beyond normal human capabilities, which they may use to fight enemies, protect others, or face unusual challenges.

MARTIAL ARTS

Martial arts manhwa focuses on hand-to-hand combat, fighting techniques, and martial arts training, often featuring rival schools, tournaments, and powerful martial artists.
