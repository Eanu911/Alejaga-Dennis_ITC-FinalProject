MY TOP 4 ONGOING ACTION MANHWA

🥇 Lookism

Tags: Action, Slice of Life, Martial Arts
Chapter Count: 632 (Ongoing)
Author(s): Park Taejun
Artist(s): Park Taejun

Set in the outskirts of Seoul, Lookism is about a boy named Daniel Park (Park Hyungseok) who is bullied because of his looks. He decides to transfer schools, only to wake up in a new body. Throughout the story he witnesses multiple cases of 'Lookism', a form of discrimination based on a person's attractiveness. As Daniel navigates this new life, he tries to uncover the mystery behind his second body.

🥈 Killer Pietro

Tags: Fantasy, Action, Superpower
Chapter Count: 155 (Ongoing)
Author(s): Kim Junghyun
Artist(s): Im Lina

The story revolves around the Glory Club's legendary killer, Pietro. After devoting his life to the crime organization, Glory Club, Pietro retires his status as a legendary killer and spends his remaining days running a quiet used bookstore. But after being ambushed and nearly killed one day by the very organization he dedicated his life to, Pietro finds his body magically reverted back to its teenage years. Now that Pietro was back to his peak physique, he sets out to settle the score with Glory Club.

🥉 Reality Quest

Tags: Fantasy, Murim, Martial Arts
Chapter Count: 225 (Ongoing)
Author(s): Joowoon Lee
Artist(s): Taesung

Dowan Ha is a bullied gamer, whose bullies force him to grind to obtain in-game items for them. After one such bully demands that Dowan obtain an impossibly rare item for him or be beaten in front of the prettiest girl in school, Dowan games day and night for a week until he drops dead from exhaustion. However, as soon as he dies, he respawns back in his classroom one week before his death. Everything is the same as it was before, except now he has all the skills he’s acquired from the games he’s played in real life! Will Dowan be able to use his newfound abilities and gamified existence to defeat his bullies?
