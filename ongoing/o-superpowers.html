🥇 Eleceed

Tags: Fantasy, Action, Superpower
Chapter Count: 425 (Ongoing)
Author(s): Son Jae-ho
Artist(s): Zhena

This series follows the adventures of Jiwoo Seo as he discovers a new world with people who also have abilities. After hiding his powers for most of his life, his coincidental encounter with Kayden introduces him to a new world with different rules. However, this new world is also dangerous, follow Jiwoo as he tries to survive and get stronger under the tutelage of Kayden.

🥈 Ordeal

Tags: Fantasy, Action, Superpowers
Chapter Count: 161 (On Hiatus)
Author(s): Bren Bristol
Artist(s): Bren Bristol

Che is just a human in a world full of superpowered "Kimyos" (meaning ‘strange’), but he's determined to become the greatest fighter to ever live. When his beloved mentor is murdered, Che swears vengeance and embarks on a mission to bring justice to his killer. But soon Che learns that the world of Kimyos is much larger than he ever imagined, and that he himself might be one -- the most powerful Kimyo on Earth, with the power of the sun itself. Will Che be able to harness his new strength, or will he burn up in the heat of this ordeal?
